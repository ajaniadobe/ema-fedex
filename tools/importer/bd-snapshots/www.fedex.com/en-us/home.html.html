No PID? No clearance. Prep now.

From November 1, 2026, EU customs authorities will enforce the mandatory requirement to provide Product Identifiers (PIDs) for all goods sold to non-VAT-registered recipients in the EU, regardless of value. To avoid customs clearance issues, you must update your shipping processes now to ensure the required information is included for every line item.
